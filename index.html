About the antivirus brands we cover

Editorial desk +1 805 637 7948

Cancel Subscription Guide is an independent publisher covering how to cancel antivirus subscriptions, request refunds, stop auto-renew and avoid tech-care scams. Each brand below has its own long-form guide — what the plan is, how billing works and the exact cancellation steps.

G

Geek Squad

Geek Squad style tech-care memberships bundle in-home visits, device diagnostics and phone helplines into a yearly fee that renews on the anniversary of purchase. Most disputes we see are about the annual renewal charge appearing months later on a bank statement. The Geek Squad cancellation guide covers the full walkthrough, including refund windows and safe-help rules.

N

Norton

Norton style antivirus suites cover malware scanning, a password manager, a VPN and identity monitoring. The introductory year is discounted and the renewal is billed at full price, which is why cancellation searches spike around month thirteen. The Norton cancellation and refund guide shows how to disable auto-renew and reclaim the charge safely.

B

Bitdefender

Bitdefender style multi-device family plans license several devices at once, so one household can end up with a desktop suite, a mobile licence and a VPN add-on billed on three different dates. The Bitdefender cancellation guide shows how to find every licence, cancel each contract and get a refund per charge.

G

Guardio

Guardio style browser-protection tools live in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox and on the phone as an app. They block phishing pages, warn about leaked credentials and clean up suspicious extensions, and they typically bill monthly. The Guardio cancellation and refund guide covers desktop and mobile cancellation, plus monthly refund tips.

Staying safe while you cancel

No antivirus company needs remote access to your machine to cancel a subscription. For safe-browsing habits and fraud reporting, see the FTC Consumer Advice on scams, the National Cybersecurity Alliance, and the CISA Secure Our World program.
